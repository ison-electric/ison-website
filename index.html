Why hire a licensed electrician

What a licence actually protects

In Ontario, only a Licensed Electrical Contractor can legally perform electrical work for hire. Here's what that means for your property.

Code compliance

All work follows the Ontario Electrical Safety Code, so installations and repairs meet provincial safety standards.

Permits & inspections

We file the ESA notification and book the inspection. That avoids problems at resale or when you make an insurance claim.

Insurance stays valid

Unlicensed work can void your home insurance. We give you documented proof that the work is compliant.

Lower fire & shock risk

Correct wire sizing, breakers and grounding keep your system from overheating and tripping.

Property value

Modern panels and copper wiring appeal to buyers and pass home inspections.

Warranty backed

Our workmanship is under warranty. If something isn't right, we come back and fix it.

Risks of hiring unlicensed

  • Voided insurance
  • Fire & shock hazards
  • Failed home inspections
  • ESA fines
  • Costly rework
  • No warranty

You can check any contractor's licence on the ESA's public Contractor Look-Up.

Hiring guide

Choosing the right electrical contractor

Verify the ESA licence

Ask for the contractor's ECRA/ESA licence number and check it on the ESA's public registry.

Confirm insurance

Ask for proof of liability insurance and WSIB coverage before any work begins.

Get it in writing

Look for an itemized quote, a clear scope and a stated warranty, not a vague lump sum.

Watch for red flags

Pressure to decide immediately, offers to skip the permit, or a quote far below the others.
